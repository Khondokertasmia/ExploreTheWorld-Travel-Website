Travel Tips for Switzerland

  • Plan your budget carefully because Switzerland can be expensive.
  • Use trains for scenic and reliable travel between cities.
  • Check mountain weather before visiting high-altitude areas.
  • Carry warm clothes even in spring or autumn.
