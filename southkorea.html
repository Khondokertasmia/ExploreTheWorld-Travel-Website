Travel Tips for South Korea

  • Use public transport because trains and buses are efficient.
  • Try local food such as Korean BBQ, bibimbap, and street snacks.
  • Learn simple Korean phrases for greetings and ordering food.
  • Visit both modern city areas and traditional cultural sites.
